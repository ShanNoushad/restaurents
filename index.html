Our story

Three rooms, one kitchen, no shortcuts.

ABC opened in a converted grain store with a wood-fired oven and a short list of people who cared about where the food came from. That hasn't changed. Our farmers deliver twice a week, our bread is still made downstairs, and the menu is rewritten as the seasons move.

We're not trying to be everything. We're trying to be very good at a small number of dishes, poured alongside wines we'd actually order ourselves.

A

Artisan

Bread, pasta and stock made in-house daily. Nothing arrives pre-made — if it's on the plate, someone here shaped it.
